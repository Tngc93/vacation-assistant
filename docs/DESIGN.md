# Design direction: "Travel journal"

Premium and niche, not a booking site. The reference is an independent travel magazine crossed with a boarding pass: warm paper, sharp ink, generous whitespace, one confident accent colour. Every screen should feel printed and calm, never busy or salesy.

## Principles

1. **Typography first.** Large serif headlines carry the character. Photos and colour support them; they don't carry the page.
2. **One accent.** Use the accent only for the main action, the selected state and the cheapest price. Everything else is ink on paper.
3. **Data reads like a ticket.** Airport codes, times, prices and coordinates use the mono font with tabular figures, so numbers line up.
4. **Honest labels look good.** "Estimated" and source labels are small, mono, uppercase captions, and part of the design rather than an afterthought.
5. **Quiet motion.** 150–250 ms ease-out, no bounce, no parallax. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Two exceptions: the home page's route map (see "Home background"), whose routes take a few seconds to draw, and the plan's fixed atmosphere and route strip (see "Plan background"), a 50 s drift and a 2 s line; no parallax, nothing tied to scrolling, and completely still with reduced motion.

## Tokens

Define these as CSS variables in `app/globals.css` and map them into the Tailwind theme. Components never use raw hex values.

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--paper` | `#F4EFE6` | `#0F1216` | page background |
| `--paper-raised` | `#FBF8F2` | `#171B21` | cards, tickets |
| `--ink` | `#16181D` | `#EDE7DC` | text, primary lines |
| `--ink-muted` | `#6B665E` | `#9C958A` | secondary text, captions |
| `--rule` | `#D9D1C3` | `#2A2F37` | hairline borders (1px) |
| `--accent` | `#B8492A` (terracotta) | `#E07650` | primary action, selection, best price |
| `--accent-ink` | `#FBF8F2` | `#0F1216` | text on accent |
| `--sea` | `#1F5560` | `#6FA8B3` | map water, info states, links |
| `--ok` | `#3F6B45` | `#86B98C` | visa-free, success |
| `--warn` | `#8F6210`¹ | `#D9A441` | visa needed, estimate warnings |

¹ Originally `#9A6A12`, which is only 4.13:1 on `--paper`. Darkened to `#8F6210` (4.67:1) to meet the WCAG AA quality bar below.

- Radius: 4px for inputs and chips, 6px for cards. No pill-shaped cards, no `rounded-2xl`.
- Shadows: none, or one very soft shadow for raised tickets (`0 1px 0 var(--rule), 0 8px 24px -16px rgb(0 0 0 / .25)`).
- Background grain: a subtle SVG noise overlay on `--paper` at about 3–4% opacity, disabled in print and when reduced motion or transparency is requested.
- Spacing: 4px base. Section gaps of at least 64px on desktop.

## Type

Load all fonts via `next/font/google` (no external requests at runtime).

| Role | Font | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | **Fraunces** (variable, use the `opsz` and `SOFT` axes) | City names, section titles, big day numerals. Tight tracking at large sizes. |
| UI and body | **Geist** | Everything else. 15–16px body, 1.5 line height. |
| Data | **Geist Mono** | IATA codes, times, prices, coordinates, source captions. `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. |

Scale (desktop): 72 / 48 / 32 / 22 / 16 / 13, with two small mono steps under it: micro 11 (the info line, the menu, the language switch) and fine 10 (photo credits, the stop numbers). On mobile the top two steps become 44 / 32, and on phones (480px and under) micro and fine both become 12: no text on a phone is under 12px (2026-11-06; Lighthouse's legible-font-size check). Desktop keeps the scale above.

## Signature components

- **Home hero** (replaced the boarding-pass search on 2026-10-22). The product opens as a planner, not a flight search: a short mono caption (the tagline), a large Fraunces question at the display size ("Nereye gidiyorsun?"), one muted line, then one raised strip with only the destination (country and city combobox), the date range and the accent "Planla" button; on desktop the three sit in one row, on mobile they stack and the button is full width. Under it a quiet inspiration row: "Keşfet:" in mono and six city chips we picked (not a measured popularity, so not "Popüler") (ink-filled when chosen); a chip picks the city and opens the calendar. No origin or passenger controls here: they belong to the plan's stay and flight sections. On desktop the hero sits a little above the middle of the first screen; the rest is paper.
- **Home background** ("Rota haritası", Berk 2026-11-05; made bolder the same day). Behind the home hero only (Turkish `/` and English `/en`, never the plan). Every value below lives in one object, `src/lib/home/config.ts` (`HOME_MAP`); tune it there.
  - **Frame:** Europe, the Middle East and North Africa, anchored right of the hero text (44°N 20°E at 75% of the width and 33% of the height, about 11 px a degree at 1440 px: `width / 130`, between 8 and 13), so the routes show in the free space right of the words and under the form; far cities (Tokyo, New York) lie outside and their routes leave the box. On phones the same region sits in the band under the search (47°N 17°E, 100 px above the bottom, 7 px a degree; the hero keeps a 176 px band there).
  - **Map:** a dot grid of the land (Natural Earth, a half-degree grid built by `npm run data:world-dots`), dense and small: one dot every half degree on desktop (~5.5 px apart, radius 1.35 px), every degree on phones (7 px, 1.25 px). Dots are `--ink` at 20% in the light theme and 25% in the dark one, so the continents read at a glance. The 68 destinations are slightly larger, stronger dots (`--ink` at 38% / 45%, radius 2.2 px).
  - **Routes:** great circles from the visitor's departure city (the remembered `va.origin` when it is one of our 68 destinations, else İstanbul; a small `--ink` ring marks it), dashed (4/4) in `--accent`, 1.5 px at 70%. Each is drawn in 3–5 s ease-in-out (longer routes take longer), with a short solid trail (70 px) behind its head that fades towards its tail and a 3.5 px `--accent` dot at the head. On arrival the dot goes out, a small ring opens from the city (to 16 px, 0.9 s, fading) and the city's code (Geist Mono, uppercase, 11 px, `--ink` with a paper halo: "ROM") shows for about 1.6 s and fades; the route holds a moment and fades. Three to four routes at once on desktop, one on phones (still labelled).
  - **Priority route:** choosing a city in "Nereye", or a "Keşfet" chip under the pointer or the focus, draws its route from the origin: 2.25 px, fully opaque, over the others. While the city stays chosen the route stays whole, with a dot at the city and both codes beside it ("IST", "ROM").
  - **Pointer:** dots within ~140 px of the pointer or a touch reach about twice their opacity at the centre and grow a little, softly towards the edge. No parallax, nothing tied to scrolling, nothing that follows the cursor.
  - **Scrim:** each line of words (tagline, title, lead, the "Keşfet:" label; the chips are opaque) carries its own `.home-scrim`: plain `--paper` under the element's own box and a few pixels around it, fading out over ~16 px, with no wide boxes over the map. The words keep the contrast they have on paper; `e2e/home-background.spec.ts` checks that they look the same with and without the map under them, in both themes.
  - **Behaviour:** Canvas 2D, `aria-hidden`, never focusable, `pointer-events: none`; it starts after the page has loaded and the browser is idle (never in the way of the first or largest paint), runs at most 30 frames a second with the device pixel ratio capped at 2, and pauses while the tab is hidden or the map is off screen. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, a data saver or `prefers-reduced-data` it is completely still: the map and the destinations at the same opacities, no routes, no loop. Not printed. Only tokens: the colours are read from `--ink`, `--accent` and `--paper` and follow the theme.
- **Plan background** ("Şehrin atmosferi", Berk 2026-11-05, revised 2026-11-06 for a continuous, premium feel). On the plan page (Turkish `/plan` and English `/en/plan`) only, and never a second map: the hero already has one. Every value below lives in one object, `src/lib/plan-background/config.ts` (`PLAN_BACKGROUND`), passed to `globals.css` as custom properties; tune it there.
  - **One fixed atmosphere for the whole page:** the city photo the masthead already shows, copied from the masthead's own `<img>` into a 256 px canvas (no new request; a canvas is never the page's largest paint), recoloured in the city's tritone and scaled up to the viewport with a light 5 px blur, so silhouettes stay soft but still read as a city. It is a `position: fixed` layer behind everything: it never moves with the scroll (no parallax, nothing tied to scrolling) and only its very slow Ken Burns drift moves it (4% zoom with a 1–1.5% pan over 50 s, back and forth, ease-in-out; on its own compositor layer, `will-change: transform`; completely still with reduced motion). It fades in (700 ms) once the photo has loaded. The site header and footer keep their own paper over it.
  - **Where it shows (a mask over the viewport):** strongest over the photo and map column, above it and at the right edge; 5% of that over the words' column on the left (horizontally 5% up to 58.5% of the width, full from 72%); vertically full over the top 35% of the viewport, easing to 65% at its bottom. At its strongest 58% in the light theme and 50% in the dark one. Phones (revised 2026-11-06): the hero and the sections are opaque cards there, so it shows only in the 24 px band between the route strip and the hero card, in the gaps between the cards and in the gutters; to be clearly there it is 56% / 48% at its strongest, with no vertical fade, 5% of that over the gaps' coordinate at the left (up to 6.5 rem) and full from 60% of the width to the right edge.
  - **The city's tone:** the photo's own colour picks the tritone's middle, one rule in `src/lib/plan-background/tone.ts`: red minus blue, each pixel weighted by its saturation; above 0 the city is warm and the middle is `--accent`, else cool and `--sea` (a grey photo is cool). The ends are always `--ink` and `--paper`: the darker one on the shadows, the lighter one on the highlights, so the page's colour never changes, only the city's tone shows. Today Barcelona is warm, Oslo and Tokyo cool. A theme change repaints the canvas.
  - **Without a photo** (none in the city's data, as Amsterdam today, or it fails to load): a plain gradient from tokens set by the city's coordinates: a `--sea` wash into `--paper` whose angle follows the longitude, under a soft light at the top, at the longitude's place across the width, warmer (part `--accent`, up to 55%) towards the equator and pure `--sea` from 60° north or south. No purple, no blue-to-pink.
  - **Section cards:** "Gün gün plan", "Konaklama", "Uçuşlar" and "Bütçe" each sit in a card: `--paper-raised`, a 1px `--rule` border, 6px corners and the one soft ticket shadow, the section title inside (padding 16 px on phones, 32 px on desktop). The atmosphere shows between the cards. Phones also put the hero in such a card (full width inside the 16 px gutter, the same 16 px padding, 24 px under the route strip); on desktop the hero keeps its open layout over the atmosphere, its words on the almost-paper left. Printed, the cards are plain paper again.
  - **No card inside a card** (2026-11-06): inside a section card nothing else is framed. Text, links, empty states and loading skeletons sit straight on the card; a sub-part is set apart by a hairline on one side at most (the AI side panel's left rule, "Son değişiklik"'s left bar). Only objects keep a frame, a 1px hairline and no second shadow: flight tickets, the budget receipt, web search results and maps (`data-object-card`). A ticket's notches show the card's paper (`--ticket-notch`). `e2e/design-polish.spec.ts` checks the rule at 375 and 1440 px, with the full flight list, the stays' web search and the AI panel open.
  - **Route strip:** 28 px under the site header, inside the content column: the origin's code, a dashed `--accent` line (1.5 px, 4/4) between a small `--ink` ring and an `--accent` dot, and the destination's code, Geist Mono at 12 px, each code a small stub on `--paper-raised` with a `--rule` border (like the carousel's rank badge) so it reads over the atmosphere ("IST · BCN"). The line draws once from left to right in 2 s ease-in-out, then stays; a new origin (`?from=`, from the flights section's "Kalkış") brings a new line, drawn again. Arrives drawn with reduced motion. A trip from the destination itself shows only its code. Hidden from assistive technology: the flights section names the origin in words.
  - **Between the cards** (the same 64 / 80 px the sections' grid gap had): a faint parallel of `--ink` at 7%, full width, with meridians every 6 rem hanging from it into the lower half (centred on the page, so they line up from gap to gap), fading at the gap's edges; and at the left edge, just above the parallel and clear of every line, the city's coordinate in mono at 12 px, `--ink-muted`, latitude and longitude in turn ("41°23′ K", "2°11′ D"; "41°23′ N", "2°11′ E" in English). Nothing sits behind a section's content.
  - **In-page menu:** the sticky menu keeps `--paper`, now across the full width, so it reads over the atmosphere.
  - **Readability:** no scrims or halos behind words. The photo credit sits inside the photo's own card (`--paper-raised`, `--rule` border); chips, cards, arrows, the map, the place details and the section cards paint their own opaque surface. `e2e/plan-background.spec.ts` serves a pure black-and-white photo (the hardest case: both tritone ends under the words) and measures, in both themes at 1440 and 375 px, the lowest contrast between each word's colour and every pixel behind its line boxes (glyphs transparent), for every text on the page above the footer, in the cards and out of them: AA (4.5:1) everywhere; without the mask it fails.
  - **Behaviour:** `aria-hidden`, never focusable, `pointer-events: none`; `z-index: -1` in the root stacking context paints it over the page's own paper and under everything else, with no stacking context of its own (the phone map's full-screen dialog must stay above the sticky menu). The full-width graticule is clipped by the page (`overflow-x: clip`, no sideways scroll). None of it is printed.

## Plan page layout

- **Plan hero (first screen).** On desktop the whole hero, under the site header, fits one viewport (`100svh` minus the header and the route strip, see "Plan background"; checked at 1280×720, 1440×900, 1512×982). Left, about 7/12: the city name in Fraunces at the headline size (48, 32 on mobile), a mono info line (dates · nights · local time · the destination currency's rate in the budget's currency — "1 EUR = 55,62 ₺", "1 EUR = 1,12 USD", none when the budget is in the destination's currency — · "Düzenle"; the people and the origin are chosen in the stay and flight sections, and "Düzenle" goes home with the city and the dates filled in), the category chips, then the places carousel filling the rest. Right, about 5/12: the Commons photo as a short duotone strip (16:7) with its credit, and the map filling the remaining height. On mobile: name, compact photo, the chips in one row that scrolls on its own (fading at the right edge), the carousel (the first card's top half on the first screen), then a 180px map strip that opens full screen on tap (close button and Escape). The hero is not printed.
- **Places carousel.** Scroll-snap, three cards at a time on desktop, one with the next card's edge on mobile; arrow buttons, keyboard arrows, a mono counter ("01 / 24"), the focus ring visible, no animation with reduced motion. A card is one button: the Commons photo over its top ~60% in the same duotone as the city photo (the credit is in the card's label and in the details, not on the card), with the rank in a small mono badge; without a photo, a large category icon and the Wikidata description (else the address) in that space, so every card has the same height; below, "MUTLAKA GÖR · 01" for sights, the name in Fraunces (never in a non-Latin script when a Latin name exists: "Tokyo Kulesi", with "東京タワー" as the small local name), the category, the walk to the centre ("MERKEZE 15 DK YÜRÜME"; for a place far from the centre the far caption instead, "MERKEZE ~6 KM") and today's hours ("BUGÜN AÇIK · 09:00–20:00"). Fiyatları burada göstermiyoruz; beğendiğin otelin gecelik fiyatını bütçene sen ekle.") and the Booking and Google Hotels links; "Web'de ara" (with an AI connection) opens under them at full width. Not printed.
- **"Uçuşlar".** On top, "KALKIŞ" (the destination combobox's look: plane icon, the city's Turkish name, its airport codes in mono at the right; the list asks the server as you type, any city with an airport) and the same "KİŞİ" stepper. Below, each airport's cheapest ticket as a quiet card (two side by side on desktop) with "Güncel fiyatı gör" and "Bütçeye ekle", the overall cheapest price in accent. With nothing cached, two calm sentences straight on the card say so and link "Güncel fiyatı gör" (Aviasales) and Google Flights for the same search; a trip from the destination city itself shows only "Kalkış ve varış aynı şehir.". Changing the origin or the people rewrites `?from=` / `?pax=` in place (no jump to the top) and the flights, the stay links and the budget follow.

## Don't

- No purple or blue-to-pink gradients, no glassmorphism, no neon.
- No emoji as icons; use Lucide line icons at 1.5px stroke.
- No stock "happy traveller" photos, no illustrations of suitcases and planes.
- Don't leave shadcn/ui or Tailwind defaults visible: restyle any component to these tokens.
- No dense dashboards. If a screen feels busy, remove things rather than shrinking them.

## Quality bar

- Design mobile first at 375px, then check at 768px and 1280px. No horizontal scroll.
- WCAG AA contrast in both themes; visible focus ring (2px `--accent`, 2px offset); everything keyboard-reachable.
- Touch targets at least 44 × 44 px on phones, without making the control look bigger: small controls (chips, steppers, the currency chips, arrows, the stops' "Wikidata" and "Çıkar", "Bütçeye ekle") carry `touch-target`, an invisible hit area centred on them. Links inside a sentence are exempt.
- Captions of " · "-separated parts break only between the parts, never inside one ("1 / KİŞİ"); times ("14:35–21:30") never break.
- Turkish formatting with `Intl` and the `tr-TR` locale: `12.450 ₺`, `€1.240` (Intl places the other symbols), `14 Kas Cum`, 24-hour times. English pages use `en-GB`: `₺12,450`, `€1,240`, `14 Nov Sat`, 24-hour times; captions stay mono uppercase ("~6 KM FROM CENTRE", "INSIDE MOSCOW KREMLIN").
- Loading states are skeleton tickets in `--rule`, never spinners in the middle of the page.
- Empty and error states are written as one calm sentence plus one action, in the same editorial voice.
